# Onboarding a collector, in person

Aim: 10–20 collectors in Brikama, including cart and tricycle operators. Allow 15 minutes each. Bring a phone with data in case theirs has none.

## With the collector

1. **Explain** what SetSetal is: a list residents search. It is not an employer, takes no payment and promises no work.
2. **On their phone,** open the address on the poster and tap **Install app** if it is offered.
3. **Join → sign in** with their number and the SMS code. If the phone is shared, tell them to sign out afterwards.
4. **Fill in the listing together.** They choose the areas and waste types; do not choose for them.
5. **Consent.** Read the tick-box aloud: "Show my phone and WhatsApp numbers to residents once my listing is approved." It is their choice. Without it residents see the listing but cannot call.
6. **Preview, then Submit for review.**
7. **Show them** "I'm collecting today", how to edit, and that "Delete my listing and account" removes everything.

## Then, as the admin

8. **Call the number on the listing** while you are with them and check that their phone rings.
9. **Approve** on the admin screen. Write the reason as what you actually checked, for example: `Met in person at Brikama market on 20 Nov; phone rang; donkey cart seen`. The collector can read this reason, and it is the record that the check happened.
10. Check the listing appears on the Find screen.

## Do not

- Sign up a collector who is not present, or tick the consent box for them.
- Write "verified", "licensed" or "official" anywhere. Those words need a defined check (decision D2).
- Collect ID numbers or photographs. The database has nowhere to keep them, on purpose.

## If the meaning of "verified" is decided later

Record the check in the approval reason in the agreed words, so the existing approvals can be told apart from the verified ones. A badge can then be built on those records.
